About Me

I’m Alex Beattie, a Mechanical Engineering student at Tufts University who is energized by building, testing, and learning through real-world projects. I enjoy turning ideas into physical prototypes, working with teams to solve messy design problems, and helping others feel confident engaging with engineering.

My interests sit at the intersection of design, fabrication, and human-centered problem solving. Whether I’m working in a makerspace, prototyping a device, or mentoring students, I’m happiest when I’m creating something useful and learning from the process.

Outside of class, I’m especially drawn to projects that combine mechanical design, electronics, and thoughtful user experience.
